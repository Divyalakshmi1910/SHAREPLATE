How SharePlate Works

A streamlined, transparent 3-step cycle ensuring safe food handling, quick pickups, and verified delivery.

1
🍲

Donors List Surplus

Restaurants, university canteens, and caterers post excess food with quantity, cooking time, pickup deadline, storage conditions, and allergen details.

2
🤝

NGOs Request & Coordinate

Nearby registered NGOs claim donations based on beneficiary capacity and arrange timely pickup before safe consumption windows expire.

3
🚚

Safe Pickup & Delivery

Donors approve the match, food is collected in safe insulated boxes, and delivered straight to shelters, community centers, and families.

Safety & Transparency First

Designed for Fast, Responsible Food Logistics

Every donation on SharePlate is documented with strict food safety parameters to protect recipients and make redistribution seamless.

⏱️

Safe Collection Windows

Automated countdown deadlines ensure cooked food is picked up while fresh and safe according to standard food safety norms.

❄️

Storage Condition Tagging

Donations specify temperature handling requirements (Hot Insulated, Refrigerated, Room Temp, Frozen) for cold-chain safety.

🏷️

Allergen Declarations

Transparent labeling of dairy, nuts, gluten, or vegan attributes allows NGOs to distribute meals responsibly to vulnerable groups.

📊

End-to-End Status Lifecycle

Track each meal real-time: from Available → Requested → Approved → Collected → Delivered.
